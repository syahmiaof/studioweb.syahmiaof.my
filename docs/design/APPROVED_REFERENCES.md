## 1. Visual Identity & Art Direction

- **Style:** High-Impact Digital Brutalism & Cinematic Editorial Web
- **Atmosphere:** Aggressive, dark industrial, high-contrast, razor-sharp typography, architectural depth, dynamic angular accents.
- **Brand Identity Mascot:** The adult man wearing a black technical windbreaker, utility trousers, sneakers, and dark angular sunglasses is a **fictional visual brand mascot**, NOT a likeness of Syahmi AOF. A tiny protected badge/annotation `"KARAKTER VISUAL JENAMA"` appears adjacent to the character in About Me.
- **Color Palette & Design Tokens:**
  - `color-ink`: `#09090B` (Dark carbon/black primary backdrop)
  - `color-surface`: `#121216` (Elevated dark brutalist card surface)
  - `color-border`: `#27272A` (Crisp industrial borders)
  - `color-concrete`: `#EEEDE8` (Off-white architectural concrete panels)
  - `color-concrete-muted`: `#D4D3CD`
  - `color-acid`: `#F3FF00` (Electric acid neon yellow — primary accent & CTA)
  - `color-acid-hover`: `#D9E500`
  - `color-cobalt`: `#103BFF` (Deep electric cobalt blue — secondary backdrop & selection highlight)
  - `color-cobalt-light`: `#3B62FF`
  - `color-white`: `#FFFFFF` (High-contrast text on dark backgrounds)
  - `color-muted`: `#A1A1AA` (Subtitles, technical specs, secondary labels)
- **Typography:**
  - Display / Headings: Ultra-bold, condensed industrial typeface (e.g. `Oswald` / `Bebas Neue` / `Syne` / custom condensed grotesque)
  - Body / UI: Clean, legible geometric sans-serif (e.g. `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`)
  - Monospace / Micro-labels: JetBrains Mono / Space Mono for coordinates, prices, metadata, timestamps.
